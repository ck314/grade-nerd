Topic 11: Exponent Laws

This topic covers the rules for manipulating expressions with exponents, which represent repeated multiplication.

  • Multiplication Rule: xᵃ ⋅ xᵇ = xᵃ⁺ᵇ
  • Division Rule: xᵃ / xᵇ = xᵃ⁻ᵇ
  • Power of a Power Rule: (xᵃ)ᵇ = xᵃᵇ
  • Negative Exponent Rule: x⁻ᵃ = 1 / xᵃ

    These laws are the foundation for understanding exponential growth and decay.
