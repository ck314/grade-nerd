Option 1: Connection to Economics (Profitability Range)

  • Concept: A business is only profitable when its Revenue is higher than its Costs (i.e., Profit > 0). The "roots" of the profit equation represent the Break-Even Points. Solving for these roots tells the business the exact range of production where they will make money. If they produce too little, they don't cover fixed costs; if they produce too much, variable costs/overtime eat the profits.

  • Example: A company's weekly profit function is modeled by P=−x2+10x−21P = -x^2 + 10x - 21P=−x2+10x−21﻿ where xxx﻿ is the number of units sold (in thousands). Determine the break-even points to find the range of profitable sales.
  • Calculation:
    1. Set Profit to 0: 0=−x2+10x−210 = -x^2 + 10x - 210=−x2+10x−21﻿
    1. Identify coefficients: a=−1a = -1a=−1﻿, b=10b = 10b=10﻿, c=−21c = -21c=−21﻿
    1. Apply Quadratic Formula:

      x=−10±102−4(−1)(−21)2(−1)x = \frac{-10 \pm \sqrt{10^2 - 4(-1)(-21)}}{2(-1)}x=2(−1)−10±102−4(−1)(−21)​​﻿

      x=−10±100−84−2x = \frac{-10 \pm \sqrt{100 - 84}}{-2}x=−2−10±100−84​​﻿

      x=−10±16−2x = \frac{-10 \pm \sqrt{16}}{-2}x=−2−10±16​​﻿

      x=−10±4−2x = \frac{-10 \pm 4}{-2}x=−2−10±4​﻿

      x=−5±2−1x = \frac{-5 \pm 2}{-1}x=−1−5±2​﻿

    1. Solve for both cases:
      • Case 1: x=−5+2−1=−3−1=3x = \frac{-5 + 2}{-1} = \frac{-3}{-1} = 3x=−1−5+2​=−1−3​=3﻿
      • Case 2: x=−5−2−1=−7−1=7x = \frac{-5 - 2}{-1} = \frac{-7}{-1} = 7x=−1−5−2​=−1−7​=7﻿
    • Conclusion: The company breaks even at 3,000 and 7,000 units. They are profitable only when sales are between these two values.
