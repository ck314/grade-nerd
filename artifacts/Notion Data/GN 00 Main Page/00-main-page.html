Summary: Triangles have special lines that help us analyze them:

  • Median: Connects a vertex to the Midpoint of the opposite side.
  • Altitude: Connects a vertex to the opposite side at a perfect 90-degree angle (the height).
  • Perpendicular Bisector: Cuts a side in half at 90 degrees but doesn't necessarily pass through a vertex.

Summary: When you draw all three special lines (medians, altitudes, etc.) of a triangle, they meet at a single point called a "center".

  • Centroid: Where Medians meet (center of gravity).
  • Circumcenter: Where Perpendicular Bisectors meet.
  • Orthocenter: Where Altitudes meet.
